Type a roll, then + to save it for reuse.

Roll notation

Basics

d20one d20 (count defaults to 1)
2d6+3two d6, plus a flat 3
d100percentile die (also d%)

Keep / drop

4d6dl1drop the lowest 1
2d20kh1keep the highest (advantage)
2d20kl1keep the lowest (disadvantage)

Exploding

d6!explode on the max roll
d6!5explode on 5 or higher
d6!>=5same, written out (also !>, !<, !<=)

Counting successes

6d10>=7how many of the 6 dice are 7+, instead of summing

Combine them

6d6!5kh3>=5explode on 5+, keep the best 3, count hits among those

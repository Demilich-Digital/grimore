You're inside a bubble right now

This window floats over every other app on your phone. Here's how to drive it.

1

Collapse it

Tap outside this window, or swipe it away, to shrink it back down to a small floating icon. It stays on screen, on top of whatever you open next.

2

Move it

Once collapsed, drag the floating icon anywhere on the screen - top, bottom, either edge. It'll stay wherever you drop it.

3

Remove it

Start dragging the floating icon - an ✕ appears near the bottom of the screen. Drag the bubble onto it and let go to dismiss it for good.

✕
4

Bring it back

Removed a bubble by mistake, or just want another tool? Open the Bubble Tools app and tap Start again - or tap the tool's notification in the shade if it's still there.
